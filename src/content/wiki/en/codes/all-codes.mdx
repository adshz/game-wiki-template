## How do I redeem codes in Anvil Quest?

Press the gear icon (settings) in the top-right corner, click **Redeem Code**, paste the code, and confirm. Codes are case-sensitive — copy them exactly from the list above.

## Why do codes stop working?

Codes expire on a schedule set by the developer (usually monthly or tied to update milestones), and some are one-per-account. If a code above fails, it likely expired since our last test — check back tomorrow; this page is updated daily.
