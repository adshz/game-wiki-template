Emberfang, the Ash Warden, is the first major boss most players encountered in Anvil Quest. This guide covers every attack pattern, the safest windows to deal damage, and the loadouts that make this fight trivial.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick tip">Any ice-imbued weapon shortens this fight by roughly a third. Frostpike is craftable two quests before the arena gate.</Callout>

Emberfang is a gatekeeper boss — if you can't beat him comfortably, you are not ready for the mid-game. The good news: he has a strict, learnable pattern.

## Phase 1: Fire Bolt Volley (100% – 66% HP)

Emberfang opens the fight by firing three fire bolts in a spread pattern. The bolts travel at medium speed and deal 180 damage on a direct hit.

**Counter**: Strafe right. All three bolts fan out left-to-right, so constant rightward movement dodges every volley without burning your dash.

## Phase 2: Burning Ground (66% – 33% HP)

At 66% HP, Emberfang slams the ground and creates expanding fire rings. Getting hit scorches you for 2 seconds — usually fatal because he follows up with a bolt volley.

<Accordion title="Phase 2 details (open if you keep dying here)">
  <p><strong>Ring timing</strong>: rings expand every 1.2s in sets of three. Jump as each ring reaches your feet — the window is 0.4s per ring.</p>
  <p><strong>Why dash fails</strong>: dash has a 6s cooldown but the follow-up volley arrives 2s after the last ring. Jump the rings, save the dash for the volley.</p>
</Accordion>

**Counter**: Jump over each ring. The timing is generous (0.4s window per ring). Save your dash for the follow-up volley, not the rings.

## Phase 3: Inferno Enrage (33% – 0% HP)

Below 33% HP, Emberfang enters enrage: movement speed +30%, bolt volley becomes five bolts instead of three, and burning ground rings spawn twice as fast.

**Counter**: Pop your frost potion at the start of Phase 3. The frost buff cancels the scorch effect entirely, removing the deadliest mechanic from the fight.

## Common Mistakes

- **Dodging into the bolts.** New players panic-dash forward and eat all three bolts. Strafe, don't dash, in Phase 1.
- **Using dash on burning ground rings.** Dash has a 6-second cooldown. You need it for the bolt follow-up. Jump the rings instead.
- **Forgetting the frost potion.** Phase 3 without frost buff is a 50/50 coinflip. With it, the fight is essentially over.
